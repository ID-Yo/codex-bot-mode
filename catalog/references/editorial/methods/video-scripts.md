# Video Scripting Frameworks

Frameworks for drafting engaging video content grounded in verified source material.

---

## Method: Short-Form Video Hooks (short-video-hooks)
- **Purpose**: Brainstorm and formulate 3 to 5 scroll-stopping hooks (visual directions and opening spoken lines) for short video platforms (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts).
- **Required Inputs**: Core topic or takeaway, primary audience, target platform.
- **Output Shape**:
  - 3 to 5 distinct hook options (e.g. contrarian hook, demonstration hook, question hook, surprise metric hook).
  - Each hook includes:
    1. **Visual Cue**: Action or text overlay for the first 0-3 seconds.
    2. **Spoken Hook Line**: Direct, punchy opening sentence (under 14 words).
- **Constraints**: Hooks must be grounded in verified source facts. Never promise information or outcomes absent from the source.
- **Fallback**: Single direct problem question if creative angles lack verified grounding.

---

## Method: Short-Form Video Script (short-video-script)
- **Purpose**: Produce a complete, timed 30 to 60-second video script (75 to 140 spoken words) with visual and spoken directions.
- **Required Inputs**: Approved hook or angle, 1 to 3 verified supporting points or demonstration steps, single verified call to action.
- **Output Shape**:
  1. **Hook (0-3s)**: Visual direction + opening spoken line.
  2. **Setup (3-10s)**: Context, problem, or why it matters.
  3. **Core Delivery (10-45s)**: Step-by-step demonstration or concise points with on-screen text cues.
  4. **CTA (45-60s)**: Clear, single action prompt (comment, save, check link).
- **Constraints**: Spoken word count must strictly stay within 75 to 140 words. Timings must be realistic for natural speech.
- **Fallback**: Bulleted talking points outline if full spoken script is unverified.

---

## Method: Long-Form Video Script (long-video-script)
- **Purpose**: Structure a 5 to 15-minute video script with defined chapter sections, visual directions, and narrative progression.
- **Required Inputs**: Complete topic outline or verified source research pack, target duration.
- **Output Shape**:
  1. **Hook & Teaser (0-30s)**: Core value proposition and outcome preview.
  2. **Intro & Roadmap (30-60s)**: Context and chapter overview.
  3. **Body Chapters (1-12m)**: Content broken into distinct, titled chapters with visual cues and graphics prompts.
  4. **Recap & CTA (Last 1-2m)**: Key takeaway summary and single destination action.
- **Constraints**: Every chapter claim must trace to verified sources. Never invent timestamps.
- **Fallback**: Detailed chapter outline without word-for-word spoken dialogue.
