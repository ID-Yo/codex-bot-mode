# Value Offer Framework

Methods for structuring commercial proposals, product announcements, and high-value service offerings based on verified project facts.

## Method: Value Offer Framing

- **Purpose**: Present an offer with clear value equation: high perceived likelihood of achievement, minimal friction, and clear deliverables.
- **Required Inputs**: Verified product/service description, target client profile, pricing/engagement model, deliverables list.
- **Structure**:
  1. **Dream Outcome**: The primary verified result or capability delivered.
  2. **Obstacles Addressed**: Specific frictions eliminated by the solution.
  3. **Deliverables & Scope**: Concrete list of what is included and what remains out of scope.
  4. **Risk Reversal**: Guarantee terms, trial period, or pilot milestones (only if authorized by the project).
  5. **Clear Call to Action (CTA)**: Single next step (e.g. contact link, inquiry form, appointment).
- **Constraints**: No unsupported claims or guarantees. All deliverables must match active project capabilities.
