# Articles and Essays

Methods for structuring blog articles, technical overviews, and thought leadership essays.

## SEO & Evidence Limitation
- **SEO & Current Evidence Rule**: SEO recommendations, keyword targets, and search intent analysis require current verifiable evidence for current search claims. Never guarantee search ranking outcomes or automatic keyword density targets without live verification.

---

## Method: Article Outline (article-outline)
- **Purpose**: Plan a comprehensive, well-structured long-form article aligned with search intent.
- **Required Inputs**: Verified topic, target audience, search intent definition.
- **Output Shape**:
  1. Title Options (3 variations: direct/clear, question-based, benefit-led).
  2. Target Audience and Search Intent definition.
  3. Outline (H2, H3 hierarchy) with bullet points summarizing the argument for each section.
  4. Key Takeaways and Internal Linking recommendations.
- **Fallback**: Basic section header list if search intent is unverified.

---

## Method: Long-form Essay (essay-longform)
- **Purpose**: In-depth, reasoned argument on an industry topic, methodology, or case study.
- **Required Inputs**: Detailed thesis, verified project evidence and retrospective facts.
- **Output Shape**:
  1. **Hook**: Direct opening identifying the subject and stakes.
  2. **Core Premise**: Clear statement of the central thesis.
  3. **Body Sections**: Logical progression of arguments, each backed by verified evidence.
  4. **Counter-argument / Nuance**: Examining boundary conditions or counter-perspectives.
  5. **Conclusion**: Pragmatic synthesis and practical implications.
- **Constraints**: Maintain clean paragraph structure and active voice. Avoid generic fluff.
- **Fallback**: Micro essay if detailed evidence is sparse.

---

## Method: Micro Essay (micro-essay)
- **Purpose**: Compact, focused essay (250-400 words) delivering a single strong insight.
- **Required Inputs**: Single strong premise, verified evidence point.
- **Output Shape**: Hook line -> Context -> The Pivot -> Actionable Rule -> Sign-off.
- **Fallback**: Short social post if context is minimal.
