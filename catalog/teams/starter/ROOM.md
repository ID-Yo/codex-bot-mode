# {{team}} room

Manager and one explicitly selected catalog specialist.
The Team Manager coordinates scope, assignments, task records and acceptance.
Specialists deliver within their own contracts; this room adds no permissions.

## Bundle acceptance exercise

Use synthetic inputs from the installed specialist PLAYBOOKs. The Manager records
a bounded task, passes it to the relevant specialist and obtains a check against
its criteria from the reviewer/verifier where present (otherwise a separately
authorized reviewer or the owner). Retain source links, limitations and requester
replies. Do not appoint extra agents automatically. For Starter, use the selected
specialist's exercise and owner acceptance.

These instructions are a template exercise, not evidence that a live team ran.
Sending, publishing, spending, scheduling and other external actions require
matching authorization. Honor the requester and artifact languages.
